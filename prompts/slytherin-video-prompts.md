# Slytherin video prompts

Same style as the Gryffindor prompts: 30 second vertical 9:16, iPhone handheld, Snapchat-story cuts. Based on the five Slytherin reference stills (black lake, Quidditch pitch at dusk, common room under the lake, Great Hall breakfast, rainy greenhouse).

## Video 1

A 30 second vertical 9:16 video shot on an iPhone, handheld, slightly shaky, snapshot energy, cut together like a Snapchat story with fast cuts every 1.5 to 3 seconds. It opens in pouring rain on the rocky shore of a black lake under a grey sky, the huge castle with its spires on the cliff behind, mist on the water. Two teenage girls in black Hogwarts robes with green and silver ties, one with long straight black hair and one with a tight slick bun, sprint along the wet rocks holding leather school bags over their heads as umbrellas, screaming and laughing. The camera runs behind them, bouncing, raindrops on the lens. One slips on the wet stone and nearly goes into the lake, the other grabs her sleeve, both collapse laughing on the rocks. Hard cut to a long cold stone corridor in the dungeons lined with green-flamed torches and dark oil paintings. The group runs full speed down the hall, robes and green and silver Slytherin scarves flying, camera held low and shaky, everything motion blurred. A boy with dark curly hair looks back at the camera mid-run grinning, mouthing "we're so late". The portraits sneer and roll their eyes as the kids race past, nobody reacts, it's normal. Hard cut to a huge gothic Great Hall, pale grey daylight through tall leaded windows, long dark tables covered in pewter goblets, milk jugs and toast, a black banner with a silver serpent hanging over the fireplace. Brown owls swoop in over the tables carrying letters. A girl with a blonde ponytail stands up, reaches both arms toward an owl, laughing, and catches a letter with a dark green wax seal mid-air. Friends cheer and grab the camera to zoom in on the seal, an owl lands in a jug of milk and splashes everyone, a boy keeps eating his toast behind a newspaper without looking up. Hard cut to a dim dungeon potions classroom, shelves of glass bottles catching candlelight, a black iron cauldron on the desk. A girl with a messy top knot leans over the cauldron, drops something in, and it erupts in a huge puff of silver-green smoke straight into her face. Cut to her face covered in soot, hair standing up, squinting. A pale blonde boy behind her points and howls with laughter, a dark-haired girl covers her mouth, shoulders shaking. Shaky zoom in on the sooty face, she starts laughing too. Quick cut to a dark wood-panelled library with tall narrow windows and shelves of old books. The same girl, soot half wiped off, sits at a long wooden desk with her face buried in her hands over open textbooks and notes. Camera films her secretly from across the table. She looks up, sees the camera, groans and throws her head back. A quill keeps writing notes by itself beside her. Cut to a Victorian glass greenhouse in cold grey rain, water streaming down the panes, the castle blurred behind the glass, a black iron sign with a silver leaf reading Herbology. Camera follows the friends from behind down the wet glass corridor, a potted plant wiggling its leaves in someone's arms, a girl with a sleek bun looks back over her shoulder and grins. Then for the last six seconds the pace drops completely. Cut to the Slytherin common room deep under the lake at night, green underwater light drifting through tall arched windows, lanterns with candles, a fire crackling in an iron grate, a black and white cat asleep on the rug. Four friends sink into worn leather sofas with green and silver blankets, one reading, one lying with her head in a friend's lap scrolling her phone, one cradling a mug of tea. The camera is calmer now, resting on the arm of the sofa, just the fire and the water. A shadow of something huge glides slowly past the window outside, nobody looks up. One friend turns and smiles at the camera. The image gets softer and darker, deep green, like a memory. Hold two seconds, cut. Cool desaturated film grain, deep green shadows, muted highlights, lens flare from the lanterns, occasional accidental blur and frames where the camera points at the floor mid-run. Everyone is a teenager, too hyped, laughing, shouting to each other. No text overlays, no logos. Mood: chaotic joy with a sudden quiet, beautiful moment at the end.

## Video 2

A 30 second vertical 9:16 video shot on an iPhone, handheld, slightly shaky, snapshot energy, cut together like a Snapchat story with fast cuts every 1.5 to 3 seconds. It opens on a grey drizzly crooked wizarding shopping street, tall leaning black brick buildings, hanging iron shop signs, a green and silver Slytherin banner swaying above. A teenage girl with a slick low bun and a green and silver scarf walks backwards filming her three friends in black Hogwarts robes pushing through the crowd, shouting over the noise, one of them carrying a brand new broomstick over his shoulder like a trophy. Whip pan up: a huge black dragon perched on a marble bank roof stretches its wings and breathes a burst of green fire into the grey sky, everyone screams and cheers, camera shakes wildly. Quick cuts: floating brooms in a dark shop window, a boy pressing his face against the glass, a bag of sweets that hisses held up to the lens. Hard cut to a Quidditch pitch at dusk below a huge castle on a cliff, tall wooden spectator towers black against the sky, green and silver banners hanging limp in the cold air. The camera is in the crowd of students, filming from below. Players in dark green jerseys shoot past on broomsticks at head height, hair whipping, one does a barrel roll right over the camera and everyone ducks and screams. Quick shaky zoom on a girl with a long dark ponytail beside the lens cheering with her whole body. A girl in a green Quidditch jersey races toward the rings, gets clipped by another broom, wobbles, and drops out of frame. The crowd gasps, the camera swings around, blurry chaos. Hard cut to a cold stone hospital wing with tall leaded windows, grey light, green curtains, a single candle on the bedside table. The same girl lies in a white iron bed, hair messy, still in her muddy green Quidditch jersey, smiling weakly. Her whole team in muddy jerseys and scarves crowds around the bed, one filming her close up, another feeding her a chocolate frog that hops off the spoon. She laughs, winces, laughs again. A black-haired friend leans in and hugs her. Quick zoom on a bottle of potion refilling itself on the table, nobody reacts. Hard cut to the pitch at blue dusk with silver and green streamers raining down, wooden spectator towers, Slytherin flags waving. The girl from the hospital bed, now bandaged but standing, is lifted onto her teammates' shoulders and hoists a silver trophy high above a sea of heads, the last light catching the metal. Everyone screaming, scarves swung in the air, camera squeezed in the middle of the crowd and jostled, a girl beside the lens laughing so hard she cries, blurry hugs, silver streamers stuck in hair, the whole team walking back across the dark grass with brooms on their shoulders and the cup held up. Cut to inside the stone Great Hall, low angle looking straight up. A shower of silver confetti falls slowly from the vaulted ceiling past two huge green Slytherin banners with silver serpents. Dozens of hands reach up into the candlelight, students spinning and shouting, silver glitter catching the candles, the camera turning slowly in the middle of it, faces flashing past. Then for the last six seconds the pace drops completely. Cut to the rocky shore of the black lake at dusk, the huge castle with its spires on the cliff behind, mist rolling over the still water, a few windows already glowing. Four friends sit on the flat rocks in a loose circle, robes pulled tight, silver confetti still stuck in their hair, a girl with long straight black hair turns and smiles at the person next to her while she talks, a boy skims a stone across the water and the ripples spread out slow. The camera is one of the friends, resting on a knee, barely moving. Ripples on the water, wind in hair, the castle fading grey into the mist. Someone laughs quietly off screen. The image gets softer and darker, slightly underexposed, like a memory. Hold two seconds, cut to black. Cool desaturated film grain, deep green shadows, muted highlights, lens flare, occasional accidental blur and whip pans. Everyone is a teenager, too hyped, laughing, shouting to each other. Real magic happens casually in the background and nobody reacts. No text overlays, no logos. Mood: the biggest day of the year, chaotic joy that ends in stillness.
