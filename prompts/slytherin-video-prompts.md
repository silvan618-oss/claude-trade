# Slytherin video prompts

Same style as the Gryffindor prompts: 30 second vertical 9:16, iPhone handheld, Snapchat-story cuts. Based on thirteen Slytherin reference stills (rainy cloister courtyard, cloister corridor, Great Hall breakfast, potions classroom, library, common room under the lake, rainy greenhouse, misty forest, crooked shopping street, Quidditch pitch under storm clouds, hospital wing, Quidditch team with trophy at dusk, black lake).

## Video 1

A 30 second vertical 9:16 video shot on an iPhone, handheld, slightly shaky, snapshot energy, cut together like a Snapchat story with fast cuts every 1.5 to 3 seconds. It opens in pouring rain in a gothic stone cloister courtyard under a heavy grey sky, tall arched windows, puddles on the grass. Two teenagers in black Hogwarts robes with green and silver ties, a girl with long wet black hair and a boy with slicked-back pale blonde hair, sprint across the wet grass holding leather school bags over their heads as umbrellas, screaming and laughing, a group of friends watching dry from under the cloister arches. The camera runs behind them, bouncing, raindrops on the lens. One slips in a puddle, catches herself, both collapse laughing. Hard cut to a long vaulted stone cloister corridor lined with gold-framed portraits and hanging iron lanterns, wet flagstones shining. The group runs full speed down the hall, robes and green and silver Slytherin scarves flying, camera held low and shaky, everything motion blurred. The blonde boy looks back at the camera mid-run with a smirk, mouthing "we're so late". The portraits sneer and roll their eyes as the kids race past, nobody reacts, it's normal. Hard cut to a huge gothic Great Hall, pale grey daylight through tall leaded windows, long dark tables covered in pewter goblets, milk jugs and toast, a black banner with a silver serpent hanging over the stone fireplace. Brown owls swoop in over the tables carrying letters. A girl with a blonde ponytail stands up, reaches both arms toward an owl, laughing, and catches a letter with a dark green wax seal mid-air. Friends cheer and grab the camera to zoom in on the seal, an owl lands in a jug of milk and splashes everyone, a boy keeps eating his toast behind a newspaper without looking up. Hard cut to a dim dungeon potions classroom, stone walls, a green Slytherin banner with a silver serpent over the desk, shelves of labelled glass jars catching candlelight, brass scales, a barred window, a black iron cauldron bubbling green. A girl with long dark hair leans over the cauldron stirring, a boy beside her reading the recipe out of an old book, she drops something in and it erupts in a huge puff of silver-green smoke straight into her face. Cut to her face covered in soot, hair standing up, squinting. The boy behind her points and howls with laughter, a dark-haired girl covers her mouth, shoulders shaking. Shaky zoom in on the sooty face, she starts laughing too. Quick cut to a dark stone library with tall leaded windows, green glass banker's lamps on long wooden tables, a green Slytherin banner on the wall, shelves of old books. The same girl, soot half wiped off, sits at the table with her head in her hand over open textbooks, a wand and a folded green and silver scarf beside her. Camera films her secretly through a leaded window pane from across the room. She looks up, sees the camera, groans and throws her head back. A quill keeps writing notes by itself beside her. Quick cut to the Slytherin common room deep under the lake, green underwater light through tall arched windows, lanterns, a fire in an iron grate, the friends collapsed on worn leather sofas under green and silver blankets, one asleep, a black and white cat on the rug, a shadow of something huge gliding past the window, nobody looks up. Cut to a Victorian glass greenhouse in cold grey rain, water streaming down the panes, the castle blurred behind the glass, a black iron sign with a silver leaf reading Herbology. Camera follows the friends from behind down the wet glass corridor, a potted plant wiggling its leaves in someone's arms, a girl with a sleek bun looks back over her shoulder and grins. Then for the last six seconds the pace drops completely. Cut to a dense dark pine forest in cold blue mist, four students walking single file down a mossy path with wicker baskets, wands out, wandtips glowing pale silver, a girl with a blonde bun at the back of the line closest to the camera. The camera is calmer now, just footsteps and dripping water. The boy in front twirls his wand and tiny silver sparks drift up and hang in the mist like fireflies. One friend turns around and smiles at the camera. The image gets softer and darker, deep green, like a memory. Hold two seconds, cut. Cool desaturated film grain, deep green shadows, muted highlights, lens flare from the lanterns, occasional accidental blur and frames where the camera points at the floor mid-run. Everyone is a teenager, too hyped, laughing, shouting to each other. No text overlays, no logos. Mood: chaotic joy with a sudden quiet, beautiful moment at the end.

## Video 2

A 30 second vertical 9:16 video shot on an iPhone, handheld, slightly shaky, snapshot energy, cut together like a Snapchat story with fast cuts every 1.5 to 3 seconds. It opens on a grey overcast crooked wizarding shopping street, narrow and half-timbered, tall leaning buildings with dark bay windows, iron lanterns on the walls, wet cobblestones, a black shop sign with a silver serpent crest hanging on an iron bracket above. A teenage girl with a slick low bun and a green and silver tie walks backwards filming her three friends in black Hogwarts robes and green scarves pushing through the crowd, shouting over the noise, one of them carrying a brand new broomstick over his shoulder like a trophy, a pale blonde boy at the back looking bored on purpose. Whip pan up: a huge black dragon perched on a marble bank roof stretches its wings and breathes a burst of green fire into the grey sky, everyone screams and cheers, camera shakes wildly. Quick cuts: floating brooms in a dark shop window, a boy pressing his face against the glass, a bag of sweets that hisses held up to the lens. Hard cut to a Quidditch pitch under heavy storm clouds below a huge castle with sharp spires on a cliff, mist rolling over the mountains, gold rings on tall poles, wooden spectator towers with green Slytherin banners. The camera is in the crowd of students, filming from below. Players in dark green jerseys hover and shoot past on broomsticks at head height, hair whipping in the wind, one does a barrel roll right over the camera and everyone ducks and screams. Quick shaky zoom on a girl with a long dark ponytail beside the lens cheering with her whole body. A girl in a green Quidditch jersey races toward the rings, gets clipped by another broom, wobbles, and drops out of frame. The crowd gasps, the camera swings around, blurry chaos. Hard cut to a cold stone hospital wing with tall leaded gothic windows, grey light, pale green curtains between white iron beds, a vase of white flowers on the bedside table. The same girl lies under a grey wool blanket, hair in a messy bun, still in her muddy green Quidditch jersey, smiling weakly. Her whole team in muddy jerseys, robes and green scarves crowds around the bed, one filming her close up, a girl peeling an apple for her, a boy holding up a small green potion bottle, another feeding her a chocolate frog that hops off the spoon. She laughs, winces, laughs again. A dark-haired friend leans in and hugs her. Quick zoom on the potion bottle refilling itself in the boy's hand, nobody reacts. Hard cut to the pitch at blue dusk with silver and green streamers raining down, wooden spectator towers black against the sky, Slytherin flags waving. The girl from the hospital bed, now bandaged but standing, is lifted onto her teammates' shoulders and hoists a silver trophy high above a sea of heads, the last light catching the metal. Everyone screaming, scarves swung in the air, camera squeezed in the middle of the crowd and jostled, a girl beside the lens laughing so hard she cries, blurry hugs, silver streamers stuck in hair, the whole team walking back across the dark grass in green jerseys with brooms on their shoulders and the cup held up, a girl with a ponytail looking back at the camera grinning. Cut to inside the stone Great Hall, low angle looking straight up. A shower of silver confetti falls slowly from the vaulted ceiling past two huge green Slytherin banners with silver serpents. Dozens of hands reach up into the candlelight, students spinning and shouting, silver glitter catching the candles, the camera turning slowly in the middle of it, faces flashing past. Then for the last six seconds the pace drops completely. Cut to the rocky shore of the black lake at dusk, the huge castle with its spires on the cliff behind, mist rolling over the still water, a few windows already glowing. Four friends sit on the flat rocks in a loose circle, robes pulled tight, silver confetti still stuck in their hair, a girl with long straight black hair turns and smiles at the person next to her while she talks, a boy skims a stone across the water and the ripples spread out slow. The camera is one of the friends, resting on a knee, barely moving. Ripples on the water, wind in hair, the castle fading grey into the mist. Someone laughs quietly off screen. The image gets softer and darker, slightly underexposed, like a memory. Hold two seconds, cut to black. Cool desaturated film grain, deep green shadows, muted highlights, lens flare, occasional accidental blur and whip pans. Everyone is a teenager, too hyped, laughing, shouting to each other. Real magic happens casually in the background and nobody reacts. No text overlays, no logos. Mood: the biggest day of the year, chaotic joy that ends in stillness.
