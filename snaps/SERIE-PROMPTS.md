# snaps from the wizarding world — Prompts

Die fünf Referenz-Snaps sind echte Handyfotos aus einem Snapchat-Screenshot. Genau das muss der Prompt sagen, sonst liefert Nano Banana einen sauberen Kino-Look. Jeder Bild-Prompt = **STIL-BLOCK + Szene + ENDE**, jeder Video-Prompt = **VIDEO-BLOCK + Bewegung**.

## Stil-Block (in jedem Bild-Prompt identisch)
```
Real candid photo taken on an old iPhone in 2015 by a teenager, later screenshotted from Snapchat. Authentic low-quality smartphone snapshot, NOT cinematic, NOT a render, NOT an illustration, no studio lighting: soft focus, heavy sensor noise, JPEG compression artefacts, slight motion blur, dull muted colours with a warm yellow cast from lamps and candles, lifted milky blacks, blown-out windows and sky, no HDR, no flash, no filter, no bokeh. Handheld careless framing: slightly tilted, people cut off at the edges, the subject very close to the camera and looking at me like a friend, blurry shoulders or objects in the foreground. Setting looks like the real film sets and the theme-park village of the wizarding-school films. Realistic ordinary teenage faces, black school robes with house-coloured lining, white shirts, grey jumpers and striped house ties.
```
## Ende (in jedem Bild-Prompt identisch)
```
Vertical 9:16, full-bleed like a phone screenshot, no text, no caption, no watermark, no visible phone, no hands of mine in frame.
```
## Video-Block (in jedem Video-Prompt identisch, Bild-zu-Video mit dem fertigen Foto)
```
Turn this photo into a 3 second handheld phone video from 2015. Keep this exact frame, colours, softness and grain; it must still look like the same low-quality photo. Only a slight hand shake and drift, no zoom, no pan, no cut, no new objects, no text. Subtle natural motion: [Bewegung der Szene]
```

**Caption:** nach dem Video als Overlay setzen (CapCut o. ä.): schwarzer Balken über die volle Breite, ca. 62 % Deckkraft, Höhe ≈ 9 % der Bildbreite, weiße Helvetica Regular in Kleinbuchstaben ≈ 4 % der Bildbreite, zentriert, Balkenmitte etwa auf halber Höhe. Nicht ins Bild generieren lassen, das Videomodell verzerrt eingebrannten Text.
