# snaps from the wizarding world — Prompts für die Serie

Schema jedes Bild-Prompts (identisch zu den fünf Originalen, nur der mittlere Szenenteil wechselt):

```
[PREFIX] + Szene + [SUFFIX]
```

**PREFIX** (unverändert lassen):
```
Candid first-person snapshot from my own eye level, as if I just raised my old iPhone and tapped the shutter. 2015 smartphone photo quality: soft focus, visible noise and slight compression, mild motion blur, uneven handheld framing with people cut off at the edges, no flash, no filter. Setting is a gothic wizarding boarding school in the style of the film sets, students in black robes with white shirts and striped house ties.
```
**SUFFIX** (unverändert lassen):
```
Vertical 9:16. No text, no captions, no watermark, no visible phone or camera, no hands in frame.
```

Caption: klein geschrieben, ein kurzer beiläufiger Satz, schwarzer halbtransparenter Balken, weiße Schrift, wie in den Originalen. Erst das saubere Bild animieren, den Caption-Balken danach über das Video legen (Videomodelle verzerren eingebrannten Text).

**Video-Prompt-Schema** (Omni Flash 1.1, Bild-zu-Video, 2–4 s):
```
Animate this photo as a short handheld phone video from 2015. Keep the exact framing, colours, softness and grain of the photo. The camera drifts and shakes very slightly as if held in one hand, no zoom, no pan, no new elements, no text. Only subtle natural motion: [SZENENBEWEGUNG].
```
